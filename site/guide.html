Starting the Game

Shadowfall is free and runs in your browser. There is nothing to install. Press Play Now and the game downloads once (about 20 MB). It plays best on a desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse, or with a gamepad.

Your heroes are saved in your browser, so come back on the same computer and browser to carry on.

Creating Your Character

Pick one of five classes, choose your look and give your hero a name. Each class plays very differently, so read their pages and pick the one that sounds like you. You can make more than one hero.

Living in the World

Quests

Folk with a mark above their heads have work for you. Your quest log keeps track of what you have taken on.

Gear and loot

Fallen foes drop coin and gear. Better gear makes you stronger, so check what you find in your bags and on your character panel.

Professions

Fish, mine, skin and cook. Learn a trade from the folk in town and make what you need yourself.

Rest

A bed at the inn mends your wounds and you wake rested, earning experience faster for a while.

Tips for New Adventurers

  • The world is dangerous. A foe a few levels above you can kill you quickly. Pick your fights.
  • Your bags are small. Sell what you do not need to the traders in town.
  • If you die, you leave a grave behind. Walk back to it to get your things.
  • Talk to everyone. Not every tale in the world is marked on your map.
