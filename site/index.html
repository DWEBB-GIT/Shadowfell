Nobody sent for a hero. They sent for iron.

A cart never reached Brackenford. Mara Venn, the smith, pays coin for word of it. You need the coin. That is how it starts.

The Game

Shadowfall is a free dark fantasy RPG in the old style, played in your browser. You are no chosen one. You are a stranger with empty pockets on a muddy road at the edge of the Blackwood, and the folk here have buried better than you. Take the work nobody else wants. Pick a side in a feud that was bloody long before you came. Learn a trade, earn a name, and ask, if you dare, why the barrow-dead have stopped sleeping.

Old school combat

A fixed isometric view, a click to choose your foe, a handful of abilities. Big beasts mark the ground before they strike. Read the signs or bleed.

Choices that stick

Quests end more than one way. Take a side and the world remembers. So do the people you crossed.

Gather and craft

Fish, mine, skin what you kill and cook what you skin. Gear drops with rolled stats, and the enchanter can make a good blade better.

Free in the browser

No download. No account. No catch. Your characters are kept safe in your browser.
