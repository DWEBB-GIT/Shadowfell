You came up the Fallen Road for work, not glory.

A blacksmith named Mara Venn will pay for word of a missing iron delivery. That is reason enough.

The Game

Shadowfall is a free, retro dark fantasy RPG that runs in your browser. You start as a nobody on a muddy road at the edge of the Blackwood, and nobody treats you like a hero. Take odd jobs, settle local feuds, learn a trade, and find out why the dead in the old barrows no longer rest.

Old school combat

A fixed isometric view, click to target, and a small bar of abilities. Big foes paint their attacks on the ground first.

Choices that stick

Quests end in more than one way, and the world remembers which side you took.

Gather and craft

Fish, mine, skin and cook. Gear drops with rolled stats, and the enchanter can make it better.

Free in the browser

No download or account. Your characters save in your browser.
