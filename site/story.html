The Story So Far

The old wars are over. The kingdom says it won. Out on the roads, nobody believes it.

Chapter IA Land Left to Rot

The King marched his armies south and most of them never came home. The roads he left behind are worn and empty, the waystones are cracked, and the Road Wardens who once kept them are a story told in taverns.

What is left is a hard country of farms and fords, of wolves in the hedges and bandits in the woods. Folk keep their doors barred after dark and their own counsel always.

Chapter IIThe Great Inlet

Where the river meets the sea lies the Great Inlet, a ragged shore of fishing villages, salt marsh and old stone. Brackenford sits at its heart: a market town of smiths and traders, where a stranger with coin is welcome and a stranger without it is watched.

Beyond the fields the Blackwood waits, crooked and lightless. The barrows on the hills are older than any crown, and lately the people who live near them do not sleep well.

Chapter IIIA Nobody on the Road

You are no chosen hero. Nobody knows your name, and nobody owes you a thing. Whatever you become, a blade for hire, a hunter, a crafter of fine steel or a name the townsfolk whisper, you will earn it one road at a time.

The tale is yours to write. Step onto the road.
