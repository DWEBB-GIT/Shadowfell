# Shadowfell questing hubs and return areas

**World placement update, 1 November 2026:** The opening now connects to 34 authored onward hubs and 109 regional/return quest prototypes. The new map has 134 quests. Terrain routes, level districts, public return areas and seven sealed dungeon approaches are placed. Full private dungeons and measured 60-hour progression remain future work. See the [world review](../world-layout-review.md). Earlier future-content wording below refers to the fuller designs, not the absence of this first placement pass.

Planned 1 November 2026. This is a review and authoring plan, not a claim these settlements have been placed. [Structured hub records](quest-hubs.json) include resident roles, local quest leads, services and return links. Existing Thornford content remains the opening prototype.

## Review of the proposed brackets

Use **1–5, 5–10, 10–16, 16–22, 22–28, 25–30, 28–35, 30–40, 40–46, 45–50, 48–50, then 50+** as overlapping *local-area* labels. Give each substantial area several distinct bases, rather than requiring one NPC cluster to supply every quest in a fourteen-level region. The enclosing region names describe geography.

The timing curve makes this distinction important. 1→5 is about 35 minutes and 5→10 about 85; 10→16 is 5 h 40 m; 16→22 is 7 h 30 m. Later, 30→40 is 12 h 30 m, 40→46 is 12 h 25 m, and 45→50 is 12 h 15 m. Those longer bands are regional chapters, not one continuous stay at a single camp. Rotate the focus between work sites, forward bases and discoveries during the chapter. Even a two-level late hub can occupy four or five hours, so it needs several quest loops and a satisfying local conclusion.

Aim for a changed focal task cluster roughly every 60–120 minutes in long bands, not a forced relocation or filler delivery at a timer. A town can remain the inn/training base while its forward quest bases change. Smaller hubs improve pacing and composition; they do **not** remove the content work needed for a 60-hour campaign.

These are candidate names and resident roles, not newly created NPC IDs. Each hub should supply a short local chapter, a few independent problems, discovery and encounters suited to its surroundings. The structured file provides three quest leads per hub. Those are briefs, not three automatically added quest records or a requirement that every hub use every quest type. Services and safe access are described separately so a forward camp does not need a duplicated full trainer/shop district.

Rillbank and Mirefen now have a sequence of settlements: entry/landing **5–10**, paired work hubs **10–13 / 13–16**, and upper hubs **16–19 / 19–22**. The world map should show those local labels. It should not send a player to a single “Rillbank 8–22” quest marker and leave them there.

Use distinct geography: barred annexes, deeper ruins, an upper cliff road or a guarded lower shaft. Provide clear landmarks, signs, NPC warnings and appropriate enemy-level cues. Keep an ordinary exit available. A later side story can invite the return, but the player should be able to recognize the dangerous route on the first visit. Do not make an early optional fetch silently require the later pocket.

The old village, early wolves and exterior crypt remain at their original levels. Visiting the new deep Bell Wing does not turn the original level-five keeper into a level-fifty opponent or invalidate the first chapter. Returning residents can acknowledge the player's earlier work; safe shops and travel links remain useful.

## Level-50 endgame

**50+ describes content after the cap. It does not mean character level 51 or enemies above 50.** Every normal enemy in these endgame pockets is level 50; every elite is also level 50 with an explicit elite designation. Boss difficulty comes from mechanics, abilities, health, group pressure and encounter design. Do not label bosses 51–53.

Rewards can advance equipment, professions, reputation, cosmetic collections and encounter access. Character XP stops at 50. Endgame time is outside the first-arrival 45/60/80-hour model. The main finale earns level 50 once; visiting the second final-route hub cannot pay a second capstone reward. Endgame return pockets are optional, separately authored content.

## Production and acceptance

Build outward from the existing opening: first one 5–10 settlement and its route, then the partner, then the 10–16 pair. A complete reachable chapter matters more than placing empty future quest markers around the continent. Choose physical coordinates only after terrain, camera visibility, safe approach, encounter spacing and route lengths are reviewed. Planned hubs presently have no runtime coordinates.

Before a hub is considered playable, verify its entrance and exit, services, honest objectives and recipient hand-ins; measure its quest loops; test a lower-edge arrival, an upper-edge arrival and a player switching routes. Check that return-pocket warnings precede danger and that ordinary regional life continues after a chapter is finished. Validate every planned route against the shared XP/time budget; do not multiply the journey by the number of available hubs.
